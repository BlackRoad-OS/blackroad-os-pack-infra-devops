# SEV2 Incident Runbook

## Purpose
Structured response for high-impact but non-critical incidents.

## Roles
- Incident Commander
- Communications
- Scribe

## Steps
1. Page Oncall Buddy and classify impact scope.
2. Validate customer scope and communicate ETA to stakeholders.
3. Coordinate mitigations with Deploy Conductor when deploys are required.
4. Monitor Beacon dashboards every 15 minutes.
5. Prepare follow-ups and assign owners before closing the incident.

## Communication Channels
- Incident channel: `#incident-{id}`
