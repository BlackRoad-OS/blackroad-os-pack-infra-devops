# SEV1 Incident Runbook

## Purpose
Provide a clear, time-bound response for highest-severity incidents.

## Roles
- Incident Commander: coordinates and makes decisions.
- Communications: updates stakeholders.
- Scribe: captures actions and timestamps.

## Steps
1. Immediately page Oncall Buddy for classification and guidance.
2. Acknowledge alerts; open shared incident channel.
3. Stabilize: stop bleed, disable risky releases, and capture Beacon signals.
4. Assign remediation owners with 15-minute check-ins.
5. Engage leadership updates on defined cadence.
6. Prepare postmortem outline while details are fresh.

## Communication Channels
- Incident channel: `#incident-{id}`
- Leadership updates: `#leadership-escalations`
